## 🔬 Core Research & Thesis Highlights

### 🎵 Advanced Deep Learning Framework for Music Genre Classification
* **Objective:** Engineered robust neural networks to resolve industry roadblocks like **genre overlap ambiguities** and **long-term temporal dynamics**.
* **Architectures:** Architected and benchmarked hybrid configurations including **FCN-LSTM**, **ResNet**, and **Bi-LSTM** models.
* **Feature Engineering:** Extracted multi-dimensional acoustic signal data involving **Chroma spectrograms**, **Tempograms**, and decoupled **Harmonic & Percussive** streams.
* **Result:** Achieved a peak validation accuracy of **91.24%** optimized for intelligent recommendation deployment.
